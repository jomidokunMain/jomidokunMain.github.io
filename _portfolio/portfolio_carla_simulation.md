## Overview

This portfolio page documents my work with the **CARLA simulator** for automated driving research, scenario development, and technical tutorial creation. My focus is on using simulation environments to create controlled driving scenarios, test automation behaviors, configure simulated sensors, and support research in intelligent transportation and human-centered automated vehicle systems.

## Motivation

Simulation provides a safe and repeatable way to study automated driving systems before moving to real-world testing. CARLA is useful for this work because it supports controllable driving environments, vehicle behavior, traffic participants, weather settings, maps, sensors, and scenario-based experimentation.

For my broader research direction, CARLA also provides a way to connect **automated vehicle behavior**, **human factors research**, **driver interaction**, and **adaptive interface design** within a controlled experimental workflow.

## My Focus

My CARLA work includes:

- Setting up and configuring CARLA with **Unreal Engine 4** and **Unreal Engine 5**
- Running CARLA through the **Python API**
- Creating custom driving scenarios for automated driving research
- Configuring vehicles, pedestrians, maps, traffic flow, and environmental conditions
- Working with simulated camera, LiDAR, radar, GNSS, and IMU sensors
- Designing scenario workflows for automated driving and human factors studies
- Documenting reproducible tutorials for students, researchers, and technical collaborators
